How to use your ledger

Set up in about a minute
01

Enter your challenge rules

Set the account size, consistency percentage, profit target, and evaluation days from your firm’s rules.

02

Log each trading day

After the day is complete, add its net P/L in dollars. Enter losses with a minus sign.

03

Read your pace and cap

Check the status, chart, and daily warnings. A result exactly at the cap remains within the limit.

04

Save or move your data

Your entries stay in this browser. Export a backup to keep a copy or import it on another device.
